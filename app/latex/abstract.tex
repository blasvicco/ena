Reinforcement learning agents trained with gradients tend to forget an environment once they adapt to a new one. We present the Evolutionary Neuroplastic Agent (ENA), a gradient-free agent for non-stationary environments that keeps a memory of specialist policies organized by the physics it detects. ENA evolves a population of small neural networks, scaling its search effort to how badly the current specialist fails, and archives proven specialists in a Hall of Fame. A sequential change detector notices when the physics change and recognizes physics seen before; for discrete control it fits per-environment linear-Gaussian dynamics models confirmed by the Predictive-Mixture CuSum, and for continuous control, where a learning population's changing behavior confounds transition-based detection, it plays a fixed wavelet-train excitation (a chirp) and reads the physics from the spectral response. Bayesian policy reuse then chooses which archived specialist to deploy, combining the detector's evidence with the observed returns. A skill ablation over \Res{ablation/n} replicates on a non-stationary CartPole shows that the skills are complementary: together they raise test reliability from \Res{ablation/ENA-NoSkill/test/rel/mean}\% to \Res{ablation/ENA-Combined/test/rel/mean}\%. The failure-driven search contributes most, and the archive helps only when environment detection organizes it. To quantify the baseline collapse of continuous parameter updates without architectural memory, we evaluate standard single-policy learners (DQN, PPO, SAC) at equal environment step budgets. On CartPole, the gradient-based baselines forget earlier environments once adapted to new ones, while ENA retains them and reaches \Res{challenge/ENA-Combined/test/rel/mean}\% test reliability over \Res{challenge/n} replicates (against \Res{challenge/SAC-Discrete/test/rel/mean}\% for SAC-Discrete) in less wall-clock time; PPO, however, adapts faster to each new regime. On continuous-control HalfCheetah, ENA retains \Res{cheetah/ENA-Combined/retention/left/mean}\% of its performance on earlier environments over \Res{cheetah/n} replicates, against \Res{cheetah/PPO/retention/left/mean}\% for PPO and \Res{cheetah/SAC/retention/left/mean}\% for SAC: while SAC attains higher absolute returns in stationary conditions, it collapses upon environmental shifts, whereas ENA's specialist preservation substantially mitigates catastrophic forgetting in the evaluated environments.
